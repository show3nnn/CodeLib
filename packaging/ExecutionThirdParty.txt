codelib offline execution guest — developer-built Alpine Linux aarch64

The guest contains Alpine Linux 3.22.8, Linux 6.12.117, GCC 14.2.0,
GNU binutils 2.44, musl 1.2.5, BusyBox and their dependencies.
They are not macOS installations; they execute only inside a disposable VM.
The exact artifacts and SHA256 are in scripts/execution_probe/image-lock.json.

Licenses include GPL-2.0-only (Linux, BusyBox), GPL-3.0-or-later
(GCC, binutils components), LGPL-2.1-or-later with GCC runtime exceptions,
MIT (musl and other components), BSD licenses, and the zlib license.
Alpine package signatures are checked before installing packages in the guest.
